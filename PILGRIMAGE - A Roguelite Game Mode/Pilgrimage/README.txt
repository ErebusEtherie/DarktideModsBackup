Pilgrimage 1.8.2

UPDATE
Custom weapon sounds are now included inside the Pilgrimage folder.
Install or update a single folder, with no separate sound-pack installation.

INSTALLATION
Extract the Pilgrimage folder into Darktide's mods folder and overwrite the
existing files. Add Pilgrimage to mod_load_order.txt below dmf and your
integration mods, then restart the game fully.

Custom sounds are included under Pilgrimage/audio. The old separate
PilgrimageHeavyAudio and PilgrimageMeltaAudio folders are no longer needed by
this version. They can be left in place or removed; do not add them to the
load order.

Darktide Mod Loader and Darktide Mod Framework are required.
Simple Audio is needed for the included custom weapon sounds only.
Without it, or with Custom Weapon Sounds disabled, native sounds are used.
EWC and compatible addons supply custom appearances, not weapon gameplay.
Realms and its SoloPlay dependency are required for experimental multiplayer.
Everyone in a multiplayer session must use the same Pilgrimage release.

SAVES
Replacing these mod files does not erase your DMF-stored progress or captures.
Runs and boon loadouts are now separate per operative; permanent progression
and Ordos remain shared. Your existing run is assigned to the first operative
loaded after migration, so load its intended owner first when updating.

STUCK MISSIONS
Dark Communion may fail to play scripted cutscenes or end after its objectives.
Other missions may also encounter scripted progression stalls.
Use /pil_skip to complete the current Pilgrimage assignment and advance.
Normal Pilgrimage completion rewards and eligible Penances count; official
Darktide mission rewards and achievements are not granted by this command.
In multiplayer, the host should use it. After the final assignment, use
/pil_leave if you remain in the level after the run is completed.

Rumbler remote detonation may still use the bash animation. It is not a
melee attack and only detonates the shooter's own live grenades.
